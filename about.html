For our team

Team Guidelines

Our team members should follow these guidelines when creating and reviewing travel recommendations:

  1. Provide accurate and useful destination information.
  2. Use clear and friendly language for travelers.
  3. Keep destination recommendations updated.
  4. Use appropriate and relevant travel images.
  5. Work together to improve the travel experience for our visitors.
